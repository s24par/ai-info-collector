# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We maintain a space where all participants can take part safely, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss constructively and with respect
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them with consideration for those affected

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or insults
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Persistently disrupting community spaces

If you encounter a problem, contact the repository maintainers privately. Reports will be reviewed promptly, and responses may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans as needed.
